Scholarly method

Evidence remains visible.

The edition keeps claims connected to passages and provenance. Its data model supports both human interpretation and machine-readable research without concealing the evidence behind the interface.

  • TEI-informed textual data
  • RDF and linked-data modelling
  • Knowledge graph and SPARQL access
  • Documented retrieval and evaluation workflows
